Privacy notice

How we handle your personal information.

Draft for club review. This notice describes how the website works. The club should review and confirm it (including the responsible party and contact details) before launch.

What we collect

Membership applications: full name, email, contact number, province, city or town, BMW model and year (optional), your reason for joining and how you heard about the club. Contact form: name, email, subject and message. Event RSVPs: name, email and optional phone number. We do not ask for ID numbers or home addresses.

Why we collect it

To review membership applications, answer enquiries and manage events and RSVPs.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Submissions are stored in a secured database (Supabase). Only authorised club administrators can read them. They are not published on the website.

Your rights

Under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you may ask to access, correct or delete your information. Contact the club using the details on the contact page.

Third parties

Videos or posts from TikTok, Instagram or Facebook are only loaded from those platforms when you choose to show them. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Responsible party

BMW Ladies Club GP — official contact details to be confirmed by the club.
