About BMW Ladies Club GP

It’s more than a drive. It’s a movement.

Who we are

A women-focused BMW community in Gauteng

BMW Ladies Club GP is a sisterhood powered by passion and performance.

The club brings together women who share an enthusiasm for BMW vehicles, automotive experiences, friendship, mentorship, community activities and charitable initiatives. Our public content features BMW gatherings, convoys, social events, charity projects and women supporting one another.

BMW Ladies Club GP cares, shares and changes lives.

Our mission

To bring women who love BMW together in a sisterhood that celebrates driving, lifts one another through mentorship, and gives back to the community through charity.

Our vision

A strong, visible community of women whose passion on the road becomes a movement for empowerment, connection and positive change.

Mission and vision wording is drafted from the club’s public messaging — the club can edit it at any time.

What we stand for

Our values

  • Sisterhood

  • Passion & performance

    A love for BMW and the experiences that come with it.

  • Mentorship

  • Integrity

  • Community

    Gatherings and celebrations that bring people together.

  • Empowerment
